Hi! I am a first-year computer science PhD student at Hamad Bin Khalifa University in Doha, Qatar, where I work as a Graduate Research Assistant on computer vision and multimodal learning.

My research runs in two directions. The first is physical reasoning from video: recovering the dynamics and physical parameters of real-world systems from monocular footage, which led to the IRIS benchmark (ECCV 2026). The second is reliability of vision–language models: label-free measures for figure and chart reading, and testing when agreement between models actually implies correctness. Both share one goal — evaluation that holds up, separating real capability from artifacts of the test set.

Previously, I received an MSc in Informatics from the Technical University of Munich, with a thesis on multimodal registration of 3D OCT and fundus images of the retina, and a BSc in Computer Science from Eötvös Loránd University. Along the way I built production AI and software systems at OneVue, SynthesEyes, BMW Group and Ericsson.

I am open to research internships for summer 2027. Feel free to get in touch!

The Gradient That Top-k Distillation Never Computes

Rasul Khanbayov, Hasan Kurban
