Hi! I am a first-year computer science PhD student at Hamad Bin Khalifa University in Doha, Qatar, where I work as a Graduate Research Assistant on computer vision and multimodal learning.

My research runs in two directions. The first is physical reasoning from video: recovering the dynamics and physical parameters of real-world systems from monocular footage, which led to the IRIS benchmark (ECCV 2026). The second is medical imaging: interpretable, interactive models that clinicians can inspect and correct, from prototype-based diagnosis with safety checks on physician feedback (GRAPE, NeurIPS 2026) to learned representations of 3D OCT for real-time surgical visualisation. Across both, I care about evaluation that holds up, including the reliability of vision–language models.

Previously, I received an MSc in Informatics from the Technical University of Munich, with a thesis on multimodal registration of 3D OCT and fundus images of the retina, and a BSc in Computer Science from Eötvös Loránd University. Along the way I built production AI and software systems at OneVue, SynthesEyes, BMW Group and Ericsson.

I am open to research internships for summer 2027. Feel free to get in touch!

Selected Publications

GRAPE: Graph-Augmented Prototype Explanations for Interactive Medical Image Diagnosis

Rasul Khanbayov, Erchin Serpedin, Hasan Kurban
Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2026
GRAPE is an interactive prototype-based classifier for medical images that models anatomical concept co-occurrence with a graph attention head, warns when physician feedback conflicts with the model's evidence, and adds new findings from a single labeled image.

IRIS: A Real-World Benchmark for Inverse Recovery and Identification of Physical Dynamic Systems from Monocular Video

Rasul Khanbayov*, Mohamed Rayan Barhdadi*, Erchin Serpedin, Hasan Kurban (* equal contribution)
European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV), 2026
IRIS is a benchmark of 240 real-world 4K, 60 fps videos of single- and multi-body dynamical systems with measured ground-truth parameters, plus a standardized protocol for recovering physical parameters and governing equations from monocular video.

Tuning Into Bias: A Computational Study of Gender Bias in Song Lyrics

Danqing Chen*, Adithi Satish*, Rasul Khanbayov*, Carolin M. Schuster, Georg Groh (* equal contribution)
Joint SIGHUM Workshop on Computational Linguistics for Cultural Heritage, Social Sciences, Humanities and Literature (LaTeCH-CLfL), 2025
A topic-modeling study of 537,553 English songs that traces a shift from romantic themes toward the sexualization of women, and measures gender bias in word embeddings with SC-WEAT across topics and genres.

Automated Knot Detection and Pairing for Wood Analysis in the Timber Industry

Guohao Lin, Shidong Pan, Rasul Khanbayov, Changxi Yang, Ani Khaloian-Sarnaghi, Andriy Kovryga
International Conference on Neural Information Processing (ICONIP), 2025
A lightweight pipeline that detects knots on high-resolution timber surface images with YOLOv8 (mAP@0.5 of 0.887) and pairs corresponding knots across board faces with a triplet network and clustering (0.85 pairing accuracy).
