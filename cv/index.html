Research experience

Graduate Research Assistant, Hamad Bin Khalifa University, Doha, Qatar — Jan. 2026 – present.
Research in computer vision and multimodal learning focused on physical reasoning from video (recovering the dynamics and physical parameters of real-world systems from monocular video, including a benchmark for inverse system identification at ECCV 2026, and motion-language Gaussian splatting for temporal scene understanding) and on label-free reliability measures for vision–language figure reading, testing when model agreement implies correctness via semantics-preserving re-rendering. Mentor undergraduate students in the lab on video agents, temporal grounding and 4D scene understanding.

Student Researcher, Technical University of Munich, Munich, Germany — Sep. 2023 – Aug. 2024.
Computer vision for wood technology: automated knot detection and pairing on high-resolution board images, with YOLOv8l reaching mAP@0.5 of 0.887 (ICONIP 2025). NLP and computational social science: a BERTopic pipeline over large-scale song lyrics and gender-bias measurement with Word2Vec and SC-WEAT (LaTeCH-CLfL 2025).
